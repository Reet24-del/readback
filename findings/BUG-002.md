# BUG-002: OTPs, PNRs, pincodes and phone numbers are read as amounts

| | |
|---|---|
| **Status** | Candidate. Found by the verbatim transcription in the full run; not yet confirmed by ear. |
| **Severity** | **Critical**: an OTP spoken as "four hundred eighty two thousand nine hundred ten" can't be typed back in, and a 10-digit PNR becomes "four **billion**…" |
| **Engine / voice** | Voice Agent API TTS via `greeting`, voice `anna` |
| **Reproducibility** | Every OTP (9/9), PNR (5/5) and pincode (5/5), plus 8 of 10 hand-written phone formats. `+91 XXXXX XXXXX` numbers were read correctly (11/11). |

## Summary
A run of digits that is a **code** (OTP, PNR, pincode, account number, phone number) is read as a **cardinal number**. Phone numbers with spaces or hyphens are read group by group as amounts, and hyphens become "minus".

## Evidence (verbatim transcription)
| Written | Spoken as |
|---|---|
| Your OTP is `482910` | four hundred eighty two thousand nine hundred ten |
| Your OTP is `033705` | thirty three thousand seven hundred five (the leading zero is gone) |
| Your PNR is `4521367890` | four billion five hundred twenty one million three hundred sixty seven thousand eight hundred ninety |
| Delivery pincode `110001` | one hundred ten thousand one |
| Call `9988776655` now | nine billion nine hundred eighty eight million… |
| `(080) 4567 8901` | eighty four thousand five hundred sixty seven eight thousand nine hundred one |
| `98200 12345` | ninety eight thousand two hundred twelve thousand three hundred forty five |
| `1800-123-4567` | eighteen hundred one hundred twenty three four thousand five hundred sixty seven |
| `+91-90000-00001` | plus ninety one **minus** ninety thousand **minus** one |
| `+91 98765 43210` ✅ | plus nine one nine eight seven six five four three two one zero |

## Why it matters
- OTP and PNR read-outs are among the most common things voice agents in India say. A caller can't reliably write down "three hundred thirty two thousand four hundred seventy four".
- Leading zeros disappear (`033705` → "thirty three thousand…"), so the code itself changes.
- "Minus" in a phone number is plainly wrong.

## Reproduce
```bash
.venv/bin/python -m readback.runner --ids code-001,code-002,code-006,phone-007,phone-008,phone-005 --voices anna
```
